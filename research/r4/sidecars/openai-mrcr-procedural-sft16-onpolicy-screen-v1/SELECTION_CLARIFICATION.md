# Selection terminology

This is a newly proposed, adaptive diagnostic condition: MAIN chose the predetermined midpoint step16 after observing the completed cp32/T.5 G4 collapse, but before any cp16 model query. It was not the original training run's primary endpoint (that remains step32), and it is not an a-priori-before-training experiment.

The native binding's inherited field `post_training_or_evaluation_checkpoint_selection: false` refers only to the absence of performance-based selection among evaluated cp16 candidate checkpoints; it must not be cited as evidence that the cp16 condition was selected before training. The explicit `selected_step:16`, `source_run_fixed_primary_step:32`, selection description and this chronology are the precise interpretation. No best-checkpoint or confirmatory-selection claim is supported.
