strong upstream, so their contrast alone would not test whether correspondence improvement reaches
the root. Before any bridge model output, V2 adds the weak labels-only baseline. The exact frozen
panel, metadata, questions, root checkpoint, seeds, prompts apart from the leaf encoding contract,
and scientific gates are unchanged. The factorial is now three leaf encodings by two questions by
two root policies by eight contexts: 24 leaf acquisitions and 96 roots, minimum 120 calls.

All three leaf requests use the released base model, not the c32 TREC adapter. This matches the model
that produced the upstream stable-anchor evidence. The qualified QS6 service demonstrably exposes
the base under its exact model-path alias in the pinned `SUITE_PREFLIGHT.json`; it also exposes QS6
for roots. This is a correspondence-package comparison, not a child-SFT comparison. Outer/owned/work
caps become 2,400/2,370/2,250 seconds. No reranking, fallback, retry, or outcome-selected arm exists.
