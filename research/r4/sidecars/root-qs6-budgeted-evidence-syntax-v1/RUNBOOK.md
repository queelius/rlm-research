# Runbook

MAIN owns GPU launches. From this directory, with one exclusive GPU in `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES` and
`STRICT_RLM_CALIBRATION_API_KEY` populated only in process memory, execute the exact `argv` in
`READY.json`. Do not retry automatically. Preserve partial rows, native request/response audits,
service logs, and `OWNER_TERMINAL.json`; release the service under the owner's existing deadline.

The two arms run sequentially on one service. Endpoint/model failures do not suppress the second arm
when the 24-row collector, zero-child-call, audit-inventory, and export-integrity boundary remains
qualified. Infrastructure or integrity failure stops the owner.
