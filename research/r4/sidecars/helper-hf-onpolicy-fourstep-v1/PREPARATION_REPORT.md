# Four-update HF sidecar preparation

The approved thin trainer is implemented in this independent external sidecar. It reuses the sealed HF-v1 rollout/replay/reward/binding functions and HF-v2 eval-mode decoder activation-storage wrapper without changing those sources. One model load and one fresh AdamW instance serve four fresh32×4 collections and up to four updates. AdamW moments persist; gradients are cleared between updates. Original strict probability gates and the128 loss denominator remain unchanged.

Five focused CPU tests cover complete global/categorical RNG restoration, committed-file/parent tamper rejection, four real tiny Qwen+PEFT collection/replay/update/checkpoint cycles, fail-before-step on changed log probability, and no step on uniform rewards. The four-cycle test observes optimizer counters0/1/2/3 during the respective new collections and1/2/3/4 afterward, saves actual adapter/config/optimizer/RNG/state/binding files after every step, and verifies each authenticated step commit. The existing actual native grammar seam is unchanged and its source pinned; sealing opens that worker to record current versions without a GPU. The exact fresh verification command, output and elapsed time are in `CPU_TESTS.json`.

Each completed group commits its original rollout, grammar masks and full post-group RNG state. Each step commits its checkpoint, qualification and collection lineage. A group without its commit or a checkpoint without its final step commit is not a qualified resume source. The initial launcher accepts only unused attempt001; no automatic resume framework was added. Source training data is exactly the original32 development records and local gold. No eval panel is loaded for training or selection.

The saved primary is step4; stopped runs do not silently substitute an earlier checkpoint. The precise independent native-evaluation contract is `EVALUATION_INTERFACE.md`; it explicitly requires step-aware four-checkpoint lineage validation and must not reuse the older step1 qualifier without adaptation. No accuracy, transfer or root-performance result is claimed by CPU readiness.

MAIN reviews `READY.json` and `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` before launch. The owner command uses the pinned training Python, `train_four.py --output <this-sidecar>/outputs/attempt-001 --cap-seconds 3300`. MAIN adds shared GPU flock and3400second external timeout. This preparation performed no GPU launch, install, production-repository edit or mutation of prior sealed artifacts.
