# Mixed-contract child continuation

Question: can one fixed 24-update continuation from exact c32 acquire the compact A/B/other
contract without losing more than two percentage points of the full-six capability?

The training stream preserves the prior study's exact 96 contexts, ordering, 1,536 source groups,
seed 994220001, optimizer settings, update count, and c32 start. Its only prospective change is a
deterministic balanced contract schedule: contexts with `index % 12 < 6` use full6 and the others
use ABO. Each of the six training pairs therefore receives eight contexts under each interface.
There is one fresh Adam/RNG trajectory and fixed checkpoints at updates 6/12/18/24; readout uses
true completed update 24, never a selected intermediate.

The run makes 192 new native calls: mixed policy through both interfaces on the exact old 48-cell
primary and 48-cell development coordinates. Same-coordinate c32, pure-full6, and pure-ABO controls
are reused from frozen receipts and never rerun. Both panels are research-exposed; the interface is
a prompt/schema/target bundle. The frozen primary promotion gate is compact improvement of at least
5 percentage points over c32 and full6 loss no greater than 2 percentage points. Failures and NULLs
remain explicit. One A100 hard cap is 3,600 seconds: training 900, readout 2,400, cleanup 300.
