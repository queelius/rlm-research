## Question

Which bounded root-training improvements survive genuinely new query compositions when scoring,
availability, optimization, and native cost are accounted for without repair?

## Why it matters

Small SFT or RL gains can reflect syntax recovery, exposure, or repeated-example copying rather than
general planning. Separating those explanations determines whether to invest in controller training,
change the training target, or focus on the interface and reducer.
