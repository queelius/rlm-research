# Native selective-recheck audit method

After exact MAIN terminal relay, first verify READY, owner completion/release, parent EXIT and the
physical request/response/result union. Rebuild all 1,280 first-pass label spans from the 40 pinned
ceiling calls and reproduce confidence/hash selections, natural overlap, repacks, seeds, bodies and
24 coordinate IDs without consulting gold. Reject any span crossing, missing call, source-pin drift,
or selection discrepancy; do not repair or replace a coordinate.

Authenticate each second-pass response against its request, model binding, single native choice,
provider ID, completion IDs/log probabilities, usage, finish branch and strict complete selected-ID
map. An unavailable batch makes the entire arm/episode NULL. An authenticated malformed batch is an
observed invalid arm/episode. Neither creates a merged map or answer; no baseline fallback or partial
salvage is allowed. For valid batches, verify every selected ID is overwritten with the new label,
all unselected IDs equal the frozen baseline, and no other ID changes.

Only after selections and native outcomes are fixed, use private host labels to report initial errors
selected/error recall, old-wrong/new-right, old-right/new-wrong, selected accuracy, merged-map
accuracy/net change, qualifying-user symmetric difference and contribution errors. Independently run
the public J1 reducer on each valid merged map and the private diagnostic reducer on host labels.
Report exact answer, absolute error, baseline repairs/regressions and confidence-versus-uniform paired
wins/losses/ties for every episode and by size. Preserve the unchanged baseline separately.

Count the 40 frozen baseline calls once as shared prior evidence and the 24 new calls as incremental
physical cost. Preserve logical-arm to physical-request hashes, provider IDs, natural selected-ID
overlap, known/unknown input/output/cache usage, elapsed time and service release. Do not infer GPU
acceptance from dispatch alone.

Apply the frozen promotion rule from the additive design amendment: adequate validity in both arms,
confidence greater merged-map net correction in at least 6/8 episodes and positive at each size,
and downstream confidence-minus-uniform net at least +2 without excess baseline-correct regression.
Cost imbalance outside 0.8–1.25 blocks efficiency claims. No p-values or independent-record claims:
the eight episodes are nested observations from four exposed clusters. Rechecking success, if any,
does not establish calibration or new-context generalization.
