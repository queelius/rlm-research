## Predeclared previous-record diagnostic

For every strictly valid call, and only as a secondary explanatory diagnostic, compare predicted label at displayed positions 2..64 with GOLD at previous displayed positions 1..63. Keep displayed-position accuracy primary. Report the 63-match count/rate separately for each dataset/context/seed/tag arm/field order, along with ordinary first-item correctness (one opportunity per valid call); first-item previous-record correctness is undefined/null, not zero. Invalid/unobserved calls have null previous-record metrics, not rescued labels.

Chance correction uses the same 63-position marginals, not a 1/K shortcut: let P_l count predicted labels at positions 2..64 and G_l count previous gold labels at positions 1..63. Expected matches under random reassignment of those predictions are `sum_l(P_l*G_l)/63`; expected rate divides again by 63. Report observed-minus-expected matches/rate, predicted/previous-gold marginals and previous-gold→predicted confusion. Also report how often adjacent current and previous gold labels repeat, and the resulting overlap of current-correct and previous-correct items. An accurate classifier can match previous gold by repeated labels alone. This descriptive marginal reference is not a randomized causal null, formal attention test, proof of previous-tag copying, or permission to realign primary scores. Present all groups, including opposing effects; never choose a favorable offset after exposure.
