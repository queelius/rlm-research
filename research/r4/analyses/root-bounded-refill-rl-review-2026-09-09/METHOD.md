# Bounded-refill RL: prospective independent scientific review

September 9, 2026. Written during CPU implementation, before READY/launch or any
new outcome inspection. MAIN approval of four windows and 3600 seconds overrides
the options' smaller two-window/2700-second alternatives. Reviewer did not author
this new campaign; shared original root/SFT/native harness authorship and known
prior experiments are disclosed. Review is bounded to material scientific validity,
not production hardening. No GPU, services, generated-code execution or author-source
edits. This method does not authorize launch or new outcome-driven choices.

## Fixed inputs and actual decision rule

Exact low-LR success-SFT8 root66cce400…; fresh RL Adam0, fixed c32 child. Authenticate
new start closure against old RESULT/SELECTION/state/member hashes, not a generic
adapter override or high-LR selection. All 128 candidates must be frozen before
sampling:16 distinct context×family groups of eight, four windows, each one32 and
one64 context with both families. Mandatory first pair length-balanced and family
assignment alternating; third/fourth order fixed. No outcome-selected replacement.
Freeze paired24 before/after development coordinates, eight per stratum; all48
denominator retained, completed strict failures0 distinct from unavailable NULL.

A window consumes a prefix of two, three or four COMPLETE eight-attempt groups
under one unchanged policy/generation. Group mixing is recomputed among admitted
binary outcomes, not raw success or selected-only rows; no extra minimum-admission
rule. Refill only while fewer than two groups are mixed. At legal stopping prefix,
all admitted episodes of every mixed group are trained, including a lone mixed
group at the four-group bound. Partial/capped/integrity-failed subgroup cannot be
smuggled in as variable cardinality. Skipped suffixes are never replayed next window.

## Likelihood, optimizer and cursor boundaries

Native causal root suffix IDs/labels/loss masks, sampling temperature, processed
behavior log probabilities, role/weight/graph/typed-child proof must remain exact.
Root actions unconstrained, child actions uncredited and observations masked. No
operator traces or synthetic qualification likelihood becomes scientific data.
Use inherited capped token-TIS/PPO numerical reduction/guards, equal episode then
root turn then current-action token—not equal prompt group. Recompute advantages
from combined authenticated native rows; never carry prior-policy episodes or
concatenate independently normalized gradients.

Exactly one optimizer step after all contributions for a nonempty legal window.
Detached current proximal probabilities are valid only for that one fresh update.
Persistent Adam and RNG chain refer to contiguous actual updates, while window
cursor advances independently. A zero-support window must not call optimizer,
decay moments, reset RNG, fabricate a checkpoint or increment Adam. Its policy and
optimizer/RNG identities remain byte-identical; no-op records are separate.
Committed steps cannot be applied twice after recovery. Final endpoint is last
committed policy0–4; no best-validation or promised-fixed4 substitution.

## Budget and failure semantics

One immutable start clock:3630 outer,3600 owned,3480 work. Training cutoff at2820,
reserving660 final service/readout plus120 cleanup. Baseline at most180 service+
480 collection. Candidate300/update240 caps clipped to remaining cutoff with
save/release allowance, and no half-window update at cap. Review actual code paths,
not only named metadata: warm service/no-op transitions, switch costs, failed
collection, save, final readout and owned release. True stage failure stops, retains
partial evidence and later unrun readout NULLs. No unsafe predecessor cleanup.

## Evidence and conclusions

Read full new sources at final hashes and small independent fixtures for legal
prefixes, real generic admission (16/24/32 raw rows), one-policy binding, no-op and
actual Adam/window cursor transitions. Reuse authenticated existing trainer proof
where applicable; no broad model test. Separate pure-function qualification from
actual native/admission/dispatch qualification. Report only material blockers with
exact functions/lines and reproducible evidence. Freeze final review against READY;
unsealed author files may change and are not a completed review.

Future outcome interpretation, if assigned: report all sampled/admitted/mixed/used
groups and root tokens, first-two versus consumed-prefix support yield, actual
updates/weights, all48 paired endpoint outcomes/nulls and total cost. Support yield
is descriptive, not a causal comparison of learned refill versus fixed sampling.
Homogeneous success is not mastery; no-op endpoint movement cannot be learning.
