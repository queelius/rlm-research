## Decision

Retain a narrow positive signal for joint scoped acquisition/finishing on two nonzero union contexts, with substantial cost and reachability regressions. Do not promote broad transfer, a scalar mechanism, or more identical SFT steps. Next priority is the already planned role/contract and fixed-start calibration evidence; it can separate avoidable harness reachability failures from a training need. If training is later justified, supervise acquisition→faithful current-state reduction→stop transitions and test free execution on disjoint, nonzero-balanced query compositions—not CE alone. No new experiment is authorized by this audit.
