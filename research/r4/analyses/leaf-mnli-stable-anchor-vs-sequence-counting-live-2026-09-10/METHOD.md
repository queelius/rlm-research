# Stable-anchor192 terminal audit method

Do not read the producer output tree until MAIN relays the exact released owner terminal and exact
parent EXIT hashes. The reader must authenticate complete/released ownership, exit 0, no timeout,
empty GPU inventory, and a completion marker matching the owner before reading scientific outcomes.

Reconstruct the 192-coordinate context-by-arm inventory and exact serialized request bodies from the
frozen producer closure. Authenticate every recorded native prompt and completion token sequence,
HTTP result, model alias, finish branch, and unique provider request ID. Independently parse the
whole output contract and score labels in displayed-record order against frozen host gold. An
authenticated malformed, incomplete, wrong-key, or tool answer is observed and known wrong with
early/late/total score zero. An unreturned, unauthenticated, or undispatched endpoint is NULL with
late score bounds `[0,32]`; it is never filled with zero.

Report planned, attempted, returned, authenticated, available, contract-valid, invalid, and NULL
counts; raw known/unknown input/output/cached token usage; all 12 relation-by-anchor cells; four
anchor summaries pooled across the three reference relations; and all 16 paired context rows. For
each keyed anchor versus labels-only, report the paired-known estimate and planned-denominator
worst/best missing-outcome bounds. Bounds are identification bounds, not confidence intervals.

Apply the frozen opaque and permuted-numeric gates independently. Promotion requires the lower gain
bound to reach 25 percentage points, the lower bound versus sequential numeric to be at least -10
points, positive lower-bound gains in at least 12 contexts, at least 15/16 available calls in every
cell, and no contract/availability disadvantage. The primary descriptive result is the late pooled
permuted-plus-opaque stable-key gain minus sequential gain; it is not itself a promotion gate.

Plot-ready output will contain late accuracy for the four anchors pooled across reference relations
and per-context paired effects. The 16 newly selected contexts exclude named prior research
inventories, but the task/model are familiar and the contexts are not guaranteed pretraining-unseen.
Contexts are the paired units; calls, records, and labels within them are dependent. Structured
decoding forces key grammar and output order, so this is an encoding-package comparison, not proof
of binding, copying, or counting.

The auditor authored the producer's two prelaunch scoring amendments (NULL bounds and observed-
invalid handling), but did not author the main experiment producer. This overlap is disclosed; the
terminal reader reimplements score parsing and aggregation rather than calling producer scoring.
There are no shared controls or reused costs in this 192-call study.
