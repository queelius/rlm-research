## Next decision and artifacts

The smallest informative next evaluation is a rotated-order, new-context comparison of matching-ID leaf requests under classifier/no-tools with and without the exact vocabulary/shape constraint. For whole-RLM transfer, change the leaf interface only while keeping root tools available, and audit leaf accuracy, returned map validity, root aggregation and full cost separately. This released-base result does not automatically establish the same effect under c32 or another trained child. A trainable correspondence objective should preserve actual input-ID/label alignment and stopping rather than teach constant tags or rely on post-hoc repair.

Do not extend generic terminal-format SFT merely because free invalidity is high: the study separates tool routing, long-array stopping and label-vocabulary adherence from semantic correspondence. Exact matching is a useful candidate, but stronger mechanism/generalization claims require new contexts and balanced order.
