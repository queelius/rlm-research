## Reporting boundaries, not requested fixes

- A completed endpoint with a provider error is deliberately training-null even if its literal final answer is correct. The separate `strict_endpoint_reward`/`strict_reward` fields retain that endpoint evidence. Future outcome analysis must not conflate `strict_successes` (admitted) with all observed exact answers, or describe every provider-null as no model output. There is no recovered-overflow amendment in this new campaign.
- All stages require complete planned recording before export; partial capped rounds do not update. A homogeneous admitted round stops without rerolls. That may be scientifically uninformative but is not a reason to invent rewards or silently continue training.
- Child semantic labels can be wrong despite valid typed maps. Endpoint reward does not certify complete classification or correct intermediate reasoning. Persistent Adam plus eight nominal updates does not alone establish question-sensitive planning.
- Reused readout seeds are paired coordinates, not deterministic trajectories. They remain nested in a small number of exposed contexts; training fresh-seed scores are not paired longitudinal controls.
- Saved wire JSON does not independently retain original schema property order. The ordered schema string and pinned pre-transport assertion are the declared provenance boundary; no stronger raw-byte claim is warranted.
- The inherited equal-episode/equal-turn/mean-action-token objective is intentionally not an unnormalized terminal-return trajectory gradient. Report it accurately; no cost-aware objective was introduced.

No hot edit or additional gate is recommended from this review. Continue the already accepted run and audit actual admission, distribution guards, update commits and terminal outcomes when complete.
