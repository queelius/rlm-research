# Pre-output primary-estimand amendment

The preserved original design assigned promotion gates to opaque minus sequential numeric. After V3
added labels-only, MAIN identified that opaque minus labels-only is the comparison that tests whether
the upstream correspondence improvement survives the downstream bridge. Before launch or any model
output, the same numerical gates are reassigned to `opaque - labels_only`. No number, input, prompt,
seed, context, model, budget, or missing-data rule changes.

`Opaque - sequential_numeric` remains an important secondary comparison of two strong keyed
interfaces, but it is descriptive/equivalence evidence and cannot retire the carry-through result.
The old design and methods remain preserved and are not silently rewritten.
