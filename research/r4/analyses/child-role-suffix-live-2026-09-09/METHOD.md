## Question and units

Does a child-only instruction reduce costly, off-task child behavior without reducing complete exact root answers? This is an exploratory, exposed, failure-enriched comparison, not a general efficiency or training result. Three context groups were selected from earlier costly failures; the fourth is an exposed moderate comparator. Four prior costly episodes represented only three groups. Fresh seeds do not remove selection effects or regression to the mean.

The frozen design is four task/context groups × two seeds × two arms = 16 episodes, eight paired coordinates, four context clusters. Groups are `training-016-00:human_being`, `training-032-00:numeric_value`, `training-064-00:entity`, and `validation-064-02:entity`. The historical split names do not imply scientific non-exposure. It is an instruction, not tool disabling. Root depth 0 must never receive the suffix. Ordinary root instructions, task/context bytes, tools, recursion and alias routing remain unchanged. Record exact source and patched-engine identities, qualification evidence and actual per-episode overlay records. Report a funnel with separate denominators:

1. Routed wrapper attempts, plus pre-routing failures.

4. Returned native responses and sampled completions, including partial/error-bearing records.

The 2,048 limit counts **allowed final request-hook entries**, not confirmed server receipt, actual inference, sampled turns or successful provider calls. A routing failure before this seam is a distinct zero-hook attempt.
