## Decision

The strongest supported signal is **strategy and cost**: withholding preprinted raw records induces canonical-file loading and avoids long literal regeneration on this exposed panel. Accuracy improvement remains small and uncertain, with one genuine order-sensitive reduction failure surviving file access. A compact external-state interface is therefore worth carrying into a bounded fresh-context/native-task transfer check or mixed producer/operator curriculum; this does not justify declaring operator competence solved, mandatory algorithm teaching, more epochs, or a novel offloading claim. Preserve source-ID/typed-state fidelity and measure successful state use alongside native final correctness.
