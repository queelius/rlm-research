# Mixed-contract SFT audit method

The reader first requires the exact owner terminal, sole exact-parent EXIT, marker hash, successful
exit, release, and empty post-exit GPU process list. It then requires all 192 planned coordinates
by unique ID, independently replays each raw native token response through the frozen protocol and
host gold, and compares replay objects/scores to call RESULT and terminal inventory. Authenticated
malformed output is observed zero; missing/unreturned/unauthenticated response is NULL.

Primary and development cells are reported separately and both are research-exposed. On the 48
primary coordinates per interface, mixed update24 is paired with exact old c32 receipts and with
the pure-full6 and pure-ABO update24 controls. The frozen promotion gate requires at least +5
percentage points over c32 under ABO (at least +39/768 labels) and no more than 2 percentage points
loss under full6 (at least -15/768 labels). NULL-sensitive lower/upper bounds and known-only paired
effects are retained; no missing cell is zero-filled.

The training audit requires the fixed-last selection to be true completed update24, milestone
checkpoints 6/12/18/24 with cursor 24/48/72/96, manifest-bound file hashes, populated finite Adam
state at the corresponding step, and Python/torch/CUDA RNG state. It records physical requests,
responses, results, choice payloads, token usage with unknown counts, training time, rollout time,
and owner/parent elapsed time. This authenticates persisted state but does not reproduce training.
