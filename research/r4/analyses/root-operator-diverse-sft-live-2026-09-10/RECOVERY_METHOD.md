# Prospective audit method: additive SFT6 completion

Frozen before recovery acceptance, launch, or sampled output. Written after the original attempt's
infrastructure failure and therefore conditional on that observed failure, but before recovery
outcomes. The original audit and manifest remain immutable.

## Scope and estimand

The recovery may complete only the previously unrun treatment arm. It must reuse the sealed 72-row
corpus, released-reference 857 start, train seed, six-example nonliteral gate, six full-corpus passes,
Adam learning rate 1e-4 and the original 24 fixed-SFT6 coordinates. Capture and unchanged readout may
not be rerun. The eventual paired estimate joins time-separated recovery SFT6 outcomes to the frozen
original unchanged outcomes; this repairs infrastructure coverage, not randomization or the small,
research-exposed panel.

## Checks fixed before outcomes

1. Verify byte identity of the original READY_v2, corpus closure, start binding, train plan, original
   failure/terminal/cost receipts, unchanged native results, and planned SFT6 coordinates.
2. Verify the recovery changes only GPU-preserving training invocation, output namespace and selected
   checkpoint binding. The collector must remain GPU-hidden; only training inherits MAIN's single
   exact assignment.
3. Recount 72 admitted examples, actual current-action token masks/target mass, nonterminal gate
   metrics, six genuine optimizer increments, checkpoint ordinal/ancestry, and fresh-Adam state.
   A loss decrease alone is not task evidence.
4. Authenticate each of the original 24 treatment finals from its native request/response chain.
   Completed wrong or malformed native finals score zero; unfinished tool branches, missing or
   inconsistent receipts are NULL. Never execute sampled code, repair an answer, substitute a
   checkpoint, or reroll a coordinate.
5. Compare SFT6 to the retained baseline on all 24 planned pairs, reporting wins/losses/ties/NULLs,
   accuracy on the planned denominator, conditional accuracy, missing-data bounds, operator/scope/
   context summaries, and zero versus nonzero gold separately.
6. Inspect execution evidence independently of scalar reward: genuine child acquisition, merging all
   width-4 pieces, scope filtering, use of live returned labels, reduction, and stopping. Literal
   calculations are valid task successes when grounded in actual observations, but are not evidence
   of learned live-variable accumulation.
7. Report new recovery physical requests/tokens and elapsed training/readout separately; count the
   original capture/baseline once in a standalone recipe total. Do not call local requests “paid” or
   infer provider billing/cache use when absent.

## Failure policy

The 24 original SFT6 slots remain NULL in the original seal. A missing gate, optimizer, fixed6
checkpoint, or authenticated recovery final remains NULL in the additive comparison. Partial
training or partial readout is retained but never substituted. Recovery failure does not alter the
original baseline or trigger another reroll.

## Independence

This auditor did not author the implementation. The auditor did identify the original attempt as
pre-training infrastructure failure and is consequently not outcome-blind about the reason for this
recovery. Scientific scores were unavailable when this method was frozen.
