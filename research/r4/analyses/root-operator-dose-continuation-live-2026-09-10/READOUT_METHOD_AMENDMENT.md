# Readout method amendment: fixed SFT6 versus SFT24

This additive amendment was frozen after the readout implementation reached CPU READY but before
the fixed checkpoint 24 existed and before any readout sampling or outcome inspection. It completes
the source contract anticipated in `METHOD.md`; it does not change the training method or select a
checkpoint by performance.

## Fixed inventory and bindings

- The full readout contains 96 planned endpoints: 48 immutable evaluation coordinates under SFT6
  and the same 48 under SFT24. The first-action diagnostic contains 24 planned endpoints: 12 fixed
  teacher-prefix probes under each policy.
- The retained SFT6 adapter is the exact checkpoint 6 control. The experimental adapter must be the
  exact authenticated checkpoint 24 selected by the continuation protocol, with all 18 committed
  links from steps 7--24 present. A partial checkpoint cannot substitute.
- The owner runs SFT6 before SFT24. Pairing controls the frozen coordinate, but phase order remains
  a temporal/service confound and is reported rather than erased.
- Evaluation groups and gold are already research-exposed or source-prepared under the named local
  inventories. They are not claimed globally unseen or independent of the broader project.

## Native admission and scoring

For every planned full endpoint, independently authenticate request identity, prompt/token IDs,
model/adapter binding, unique native choice, finish branch, completion text or authenticated tool
envelope, and usage. A genuine completed final is scored only by the exact frozen `Answer: N`
contract. Wrong, malformed, or empty genuine finals are observed zeros. Missing records, transport
failures, incomplete/outstanding tool branches, and inconsistent native evidence are NULL, retained
on the planned denominator with bounds. No tool is executed by the auditor and no answer is repaired.

Primary comparison is paired strict correctness for SFT24 versus SFT6, with planned-denominator and
jointly-available views. Report operator, scope, context, source-exposure, and zero/nonzero-gold
strata. Separately audit whether traces actually acquire child outputs, preserve returned labels,
combine width, reduce the requested scope, recover from errors, and stop. Literal computation from
authentic returned values may be task success even when it is not live-variable accumulation.

For first-action probes, authenticate the native request and returned token/logprob evidence against
the frozen teacher prefix. Classify only syntactic action intent. These probes execute no child and
therefore cannot establish acquisition, faithful reduction, or task correctness.

## Cost and interpretation

Preserve all 120 planned slots and all physical request/token/time records. Keep endpoint
availability, strict correctness, behavioral dataflow, and syntactic intent separate. Historical
teacher capture, continuation optimization, post6/post24 NLL diagnostics, full readout, and probes
have separate costs; local provider billing is not inferred. The comparison estimates dose on the
same 72-trajectory corpus and fixed small panels, not data breadth or generic operator competence.
