## Open questions that would change the next decision

1. Does indexed output preserve correspondence on a vocabulary/task not used in the TREC training, or does it mainly reconnect known labels to known question forms?
2. Does the output-scope effect hold at original weights? That pending comparison can distinguish an SFT-specific convention from a broader model behavior.
3. In full16, is the dominant failure ignoring the requested subset, or a more general effect of irrelevant visible inputs? The first-visible16 match is post-hoc diagnostic evidence only; a predeclared selection intervention is needed.
4. At a fixed root prefix, can correct child evidence reliably change the root answer? If not, is the loss in parsing, aggregation or commitment?
5. Can a proposed process reward identify meaningful failure without requiring a specific recursion shape or excluding difficult policy-induced errors?
6. Does a contemporaneously controlled, replicated root campaign improve a fresh-context endpoint? The current last, evaluated, selected and final-checkpoint notions are not interchangeable.
7. Are public-source gains robust on independently acquired data with explicit underlying licensing? Dataset-loader licensing and source accessibility do not answer that question.
