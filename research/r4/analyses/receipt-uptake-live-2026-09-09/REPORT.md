# Teaching the helper did not improve final answers; both child output and root consumption failed

Complete terminal-only independent results audit, 2026-09-09. All 96 planned episodes finished and remained scoreable. No episode was replaced, repaired, censored by the budget, or converted from a recovered provider error to an infrastructure null. This audit is independent of the experiment's built-in projection; its author prepared parts of the research infrastructure and does not claim a fresh independent review of every inherited source file.

## What this changes

The familiar prompt package still works substantially better than the taught helper packages here. Historical step8 root scores **9/12 unchanged, versus 1/12 taught, 1/12 with the original procedure restored, and 0/12 with receipts**. Original root scores 4/12, 2/12, 0/12, 0/12 respectively. Restoring the procedure did not produce a reliable increase in native-corroborated helper uptake, and receipts did not improve final accuracy.

This is **not simply refusal to use the helper**. There are 27 episodes with corroborated uptake, all extending beyond the four-record example. Their 127 distinct matched child invocations produce only 61 usable complete maps. Yet a complete map is also insufficient: a directly linked episode receives all 64 labels with the correct aggregate of ten, fails to expose that aggregate in its tool observation, then answers zero. Root interface training alone is therefore not established as the appropriate next remedy; neither is child formatting alone sufficient.

These are six exposed development contexts, not a new generalization benchmark. Primary pairing is 12 task/seed units within each root, nested in six contexts. Treat invocation counts as descriptive process evidence, not additional independent experimental trials.

## Where the child contract fails

The post-outcome refinement in [finalize_uptake.py](../../../../ARTIFACTS.md#unpublished-files "Not published: finalize_uptake.py") deduplicates by trusted child invocation, preserving the original many-to-many claim/native relation. All 127 matched invocations have one consistent reconstructed request specification. It parses the actual native terminal answer against the requested IDs and vocabulary, and checks the last physical attempt before accepting an earlier sampled answer. This supplements, rather than changes, the predeclared literal helper-result matching endpoint.

| Original R | 14 | 8 | 2 | 4 | 0 |

Thus 61/127 (48.0%) provide a complete duplicate-free canonical ID→label object for their requested subset. Structure is not semantic correctness. There are 127 request claims and 126 result claims, with no duplicate claim call IDs; all 61 valid result claims match actual normal-stop native answers. Four of those claims have two candidate native terminals, so a unique causal claim→dispatch linkage is not assumed. The invocation endpoint resolves the actual distinct native calls without silently choosing among claims.

The other 65 result claims contain empty raw answers; their native final messages have no content. The inherited nano engine returns `msg.content or ""` when there are no parsed tool calls ([engine lines 459–462](../../../../ARTIFACTS.md#unpublished-files "Not published: /project/alex_phd/research-cache/2026-09-08-literature/leaf-contract.7HPUr5/nano__engine.py:459")). They still contain sampled tokens. Targeted wire inspection shows sampled Python-tool attempts, including length-exhausted repeated code; do not summarize all these as ordinary malformed label JSON or a transport outage. This audit does not exhaustively classify every tool-parser rejection.

Batch sizes are chosen adaptively, not randomized: valid maps at sizes 4/5/10/14/20/50/64 are 8/10, 23/36, 26/47, 1/5, 1/3, 1/9 and 1/17 respectively. This suggests a length/contract vulnerability but is not an unbiased batch-size effect. The complete per-invocation records, raw paths and retained maps are in [HELPER_INVOCATIONS](../../../../ARTIFACTS.md#unpublished-files "Not published: HELPER_INVOCATIONS.json").

## Decision-relevant next step and limits

Do not promote a root-interface-SFT remedy on this result alone. First qualify the **actual native child contract** on a tiny frozen subset of these requests: can it return complete maps, with tools/renderer/output constraints explicitly declared? Prior component grammar benefits do not automatically apply to this unconstrained recursive child. A bounded fixed-root comparison of the existing contract against an explicitly declared reliable map-output contract would separate child usability from root adaptation; never silently repair missing labels.

If child maps become reliable but root still omits imports, fails to display the aggregate, or counts incomplete coverage, a small native-prefix-correct interface SFT comparison becomes more interpretable. Measure complete-map coverage, observable aggregation and strict final score separately. Operator-authored demonstrations would be supervised targets, not sampled root likelihood or RL credit. Keep any later cost-aware objective separate from terminal-reward RLVR.

Do not infer tool-choice learning from the presence of a first tool attempt: the historical first-action prefill claim was separately corrected in [the additive prefix note](../root-first-action-screen-2026-09-09/PREFILL_CORRECTION.md). Worked examples are instructions, not forced sampled prefixes.

This study cannot isolate every package difference, establish causal mediation, generalize beyond six repeatedly exposed leaf-training-supported contexts, or remove root-by-service-period effects. Receipt availability includes documentation; adaptive batch size and paths are outcome-dependent. Endpoint reward can hide partial evidence even when correct. Per-item map semantic accuracy was not exhaustively audited here. The three cases illustrate mechanisms without estimating their prevalence among all 96 episodes. No result here authorizes a rerun, training launch or data reservation.
