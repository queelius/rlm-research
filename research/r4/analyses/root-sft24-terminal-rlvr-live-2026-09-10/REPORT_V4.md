## Decision and artifacts

Do not extend this same primitive terminal-RL recipe solely because training reward is accessible or later windows become all-positive. The stronger next question is whether explicit, genuinely executed varied operator/scope/threshold demonstrations can teach the missing task binding, with primitive retention and protected-context checks. The approved new question-sensitive SFT allocation addresses that question; it must not be interpreted as selected-checkpoint evidence from this readout. A counterfactual terminal-RL comparison needs its own fixed data/cost design, not a historical percentage comparison.
