## Decision

Do not interpret10vs7 as learned/used canonical restoration, and do not infer that restoration would fail if actually exercised. Optional uptake is1/24, so the conditional mechanism is effectively untested. Prioritize the intended-scaffold published-model reference and disjoint reachability evidence over more optional helper availability on this panel. A later forced initial load or explicit repair-state test would answer a different, openly scaffolded question and must not be labeled this free optional-access comparison.
