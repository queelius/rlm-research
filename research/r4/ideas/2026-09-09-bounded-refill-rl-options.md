## Recommendation

Run at most four candidate windows, each beginning with two new, predeclared prompt groups of eight episodes. Under the same unchanged policy, add a third then fourth group only while fewer than two groups have mixed admitted binary rewards. At the four-group bound, use every admitted episode from every mixed group, even if only one mixed group remains. Apply exactly one optimizer update per nonempty window. With no mixed group, record a genuine no-op and advance to the next predeclared window; never call Adam on zero loss. Maximum: 128 training attempts and four actual updates, not an unlimited search for four successful updates.

The initial policy should be the already available, fixed-final-eight success-SFT adapter, chosen now for availability rather than readout performance: `sidecars/root-success-trajectory-sft-v1/outputs/attempt-001/training/checkpoint-0008`, adapter SHA256 `66cce4009628ff7d8e047f08e6fb3a29febdbf8fb3ed8303871f69860f1151d5`. Its SELECTION records fixed final8 and completed eight steps. Start **fresh RL Adam at step zero**, not cross-entropy optimizer moments. Fixed child remains c32de. No automatic fallback. An explicitly different proposal could resume RL7 (`root-adaptive-rlvr-v1/outputs/attempt-001/round-07/training/checkpoint-7`, adapter `809fc46ed50d36c5e61b2e8ae785ae84083b509fcb8bdd3b1914eaca3c0c842a`), but must declare whether Adam7 continues or is reset; it is not interchangeable with this proposal.

## Alternatives and inference

| Procedure | Training maximum | Advantage | Limitation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Preferred: 2→4 groups, target two mixed | 128 | Measures bounded rescue of zero/low-support windows; can avoid unnecessary collection | Adaptive support and difficulty selection; variable batch size |
| Fixed broad four groups, then mixed filtering/no-op | 128 | Fixed coverage and simple scheduling | Always pays for all four groups; still conditions gradients on mixed outcomes |
| Fixed two groups, no-op rather than stop | 64 | Cheapest test of continuing past a uniform window | Lower chance of informative support; no direct refill test |

One pilot cannot causally compare the trained policies of these algorithms. Report the first-two-group versus final-window eligibility difference as a shadow **support-yield** diagnostic, not a counterfactual learning effect. Homogeneous successes are not evidence of mastery; homogeneous failures are not a gradient direction under this objective.

## Decision rule

Promote a second independently seeded pilot only if refill actually supplies additional mixed support within the cap, all update/likelihood checks pass, and final readout is directionally useful without hiding nulls or support concentration. Retire refill under this budget if most added cost still yields homogeneous groups; that motivates a separately declared curriculum/objective study, not new reward code inside this run. A positive result establishes feasibility of this bounded training procedure on exposed tasks, not that refill beats broad batching, solves adaptive planning, or generalizes beyond the fixed pool.
