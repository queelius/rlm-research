## What the primary evidence does—and does not—buy

**Recuris:** frozen weights, evolving skills/state/invocation/checkers, with validation-gated edits. Retail's matched ablation is base58.1%, WM-only82.0%, all-eight-skills-every-turn65.6%, targeted/deferred-call83.6%; this bundles timing, content selection, context load and action deferral, not pure retrieval. The WM-only contrast is especially important. SkillFlow templates are selected within their own task families without a held-out task split. Test-time adaptation explicitly uses verifier failure bits and retries; the budget-matched gain over frozen-memory retries is small and unresolved. Tokens/episode and tokens/success differ; injected text is absent from message logs and dollar cost unavailable. [Methods, ablations and appendices](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24876v1).

The [official implementation](https://github.com/Gen-Verse/Recuris/tree/7d3745ab787b1206ccd981cb1511100476097266) makes intervention strength concrete: `ExecutionGate` can reject a text draft and demand the tool action; anti-escalation can add procedural guides. These are active control changes, not evidence that passive memory fixes our root. Read-only inspection of two complete checker files; no clone/install/execution. Apache-2.0 license identified; code hashes in YAML.

**RLMOpt:** GPT-5.1 proposes text/component candidates; a deterministic harness evaluates/selects them for GPT-4.1/4o-mini tasks. Its system bundles adaptive search, per-field floors and demonstrations; BFCL's comparator lacks the same demonstration component. Seed/polish/scoring calls sit outside nominal search budget; some polish candidates add training-gold examples. Categorical scoring permits correct prefixes with explanation, unlike our strict final. Across11 comparisons it wins9, but Chia's mean favors GEPA; margins and tiny-validation overfitting limit interpretation. Authors explicitly leave isolated search-policy attribution open. [Methods, evaluation, limitations and selection rules](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10471v1). No official source repository was verified through the paper, [author organization page](https://autonomize.ai/insights/rlmopt-explained/) and bounded exact-name searches; do not substitute an unrelated optimizer repository.

Reuse these principles, not their frameworks or a new population/search controller. An exact scalar verifier does not prove a requested operation was executed. Nor may child-label predictions become completion certificates for semantic correctness.
