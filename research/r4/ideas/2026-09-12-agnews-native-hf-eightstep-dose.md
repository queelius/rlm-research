## Unchanged learning and stop conditions

Native temperature 0.5 and concurrency 4; HF SDPA, BF16 base/FP32 trainable LoRA, dropout off, no KV cache, nonreentrant activation-checkpointed gradient forward. Reward is fraction correct over four, scaled by four for count RLOO; four actions/group; denominator **128**, including zero-advantage groups. Same detached, unclipped full-sequence importance weights, LR `1e-5`, AdamW weight decay 0, gradient clip 1. This is fresh native behavior plus HF importance correction, not identical native/HF sampling distributions.

For **every step**, require all 128 valid raw actions/masks/rewards and unchanged support/finite checks, ESS ≥102.4 and maximum normalized weight ≤0.1, then all 128 gradient replays within token `1e-5` and sequence `1e-4` tolerance before any optimizer step. Check zero-advantage samples too; only their backwards may be skipped. All-zero advantages imply NO_UPDATE. Any collection, binding, importance or replay failure stops the dose without a step or gate relaxation; preserve the last committed checkpoint, but do not report it as the step-8 endpoint. Intent-without-completion remains uncertain and ineligible, not an assumed successful step.

Two bounded fixtures would be sufficient preparation: (a) two sequential tiny real-HF updates, with save/reload/Adam/RNG continuation equal to an uninterrupted reference, checking all LoRA gradients, optimizer moments/counters and RNG; (b) actual four-key native raw/mask replay with parent-binding mismatch and no-step qualification-failure branches. No broad tests or production resume infrastructure.
