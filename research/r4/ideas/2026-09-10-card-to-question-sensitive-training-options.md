## Recommendation and decision boundary

If both learning and card evidence fail under independent closure, prefer the queued observation/harness work over either training job.

## What would actually change?

The old72 corpus teaches only count/distinct-user/weight primitives. `od_protocol.reduction` emits the particular user list and target as literals; the operator is selected on the host when constructing the target. There is no task.json in its common four-file interface. Its four wording/layout rotations are legal multimodal demonstrations, not instructions to obey a hidden batch width. Old24 means 24 complete passes over the same72 trajectories, not24 kinds of reasoning. The final continuation audit reports 22,847 current-action tokens/324 turns per pass and3,658.48s for18 updates; falling CE did not establish free composed execution.

The proposed change teaches the three *previously untaught* composed transitions, varied parameters, and explicit primitive retention examples. Public inputs remain the same accurate coding role and file-only `records.json`, `context.txt`, `query.txt`, `batch_contract.py`, with public id/user/text/weight, c32 typed child and native integer final. No procedural card, task.json, generated map, formula, example or extra helper is added to the root prompt. Thus any gain is a training-package effect, not card availability at test time.

## Primary-method checks, overlap and limitations

[Fu & Liu, arXiv2605.04920v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.04920v1): read methods§3, setup§4, main/ablation analyses and AppendixA's split table, not just the abstract. They SFT-warmstart Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct/Llama3.1-8B-Instruct then use GRPO (eight samples, temperature.6, batch8, lr1e-6). Binary exact-match and shaped structural rewards are separate arms. Tasks are SCAN/COGS/GeoQuery/CFQ; GeoQuery template/length and CFQ MCD splits differ from our context/operator splits. The viewed setup/appendix gives no transferable GPU-walltime/update schedule. Its outcome-optimization premise overlaps OptionB; it does not validate our agent's acquisition, native credit assignment or recipe. No shaping or asserted superiority is imported.

[Nourbakhsh et al., ACL2023 CounterComp](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.834.pdf): read§§3–5,§6.4, limitations and AppendicesA–C/algorithm. This is auxiliary operator-sensitive triplet learning, not terminal RL: positive/negative operator-sequence pools, five pairs/anchor, edit-distance margin andλ.4. Experiments use RoBERTa-large-encoded FinQANet/TAGOP/PVN,50epochs, Adam5e-5,8×T4-16GB; main results supply gold facts, with a retrieved-facts appendix. Four financial/table QA datasets and unseen FinQA-dev operator combinations test different transfer axes. Its component contrast motivates our parameter tests, but implementing its hidden-state loss would add an unqualified objective and gold-program assumptions. We do not do that. No novelty claim follows from either paper, and neither paper's attention analysis proves a causal internal mechanism here.
