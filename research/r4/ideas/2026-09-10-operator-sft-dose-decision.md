## Ranked alternatives and decision rule

1. **Fixed6→24 dose plus diagnostics (default).** A gain must include nonzero answers and executed acquisition/faithful reduction across multiple contexts, not merely a lower teacher loss or more zero finals. Post24 fits teacher first actions but still fails native teacher-context initiation: pivot to the served action/interface distribution. Teacher-context initiation works but new free tasks do not: prioritize state/prompt/operator distribution coverage. Supported scopes improve but held-out scopes do not: operator composition, not acquisition, is the next target. Teacher fitting still poor at24: optimization granularity remains live; do not automatically extend to32.
2. **Matched-exposure smaller minibatch comparator.** Separately branch from the same6 state for the same18 corpus passes/order, batches12 trajectories, six Adam updates/pass, fixed final Adam114. Same411246 targets and roughly61–70min training; another paired readout adds roughly20–40min. This tests the batch/step-frequency package, including clipping and cumulative optimizer movement; it is not a pure noise ablation or an equal-update comparison. Keep LR fixed and report this distinction. Prefer this over unbounded32 continuation if full-batch24 still underfits.
3. **Execution-grounded acquisition/error-recovery corpus.** Rank after the diagnostic, not bundled into dose. Freeze36 on-policy attempts on training-only contexts, preserve errors, and author verified continuations from genuine retained native states using actual c32 predictions, never evaluation gold. Include first-acquisition repairs because current policy fails before obtaining labels; a corpus only of post-acquisition recovery states would miss this failure. Select no evaluation failures for training. Predeclare token/exposure budget, genuine optimizer schedule and a disjoint readout; estimated15–30min acquisition plus60–90min training and20–40min evaluation, uncertain until CPU preparation. If fixed24 fits and freely initiates but loses state after errors, this becomes rank1. Retire the narrow unchanged recipe if teacher fitting improves without any acquisition/faithful-reduction improvement; do not describe another low-step repeat as a substantive curriculum.
