- **Broad nonzero free gains with observed acquisition/reduction/stop:** prioritize Job1 as independent harness transfer; do not credit CE decrease or immediately repeat training. A later fixedSFT6-versus857 transfer panel can be proposed separately, without choosing checkpoints from outcomes.
